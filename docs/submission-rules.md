# Stream submission rules

A stream is accepted when it meets **all** of these.

## Content

- Shows nature or wildlife: animals, habitats, weather, night sky, geology.
- Is live, or a continuous loop of live footage. No pre-recorded content presented as live.
- Is public. Do not submit a private camera or one without the owner's permission.

## People and privacy

- No identifiable people in frame (faces, license plates, private homes).
- Wide landscape shots that happen to include distant people are fine.

## Wildlife safety

- Do not publish the exact location of nests, dens or roosts for sensitive or at-risk species. Provide an approximate location (for example, the nearest town or region) instead.
- Reviewers may fuzz coordinates before publishing.

## Source and rights

- You own the stream, or the owner has made it public and allows embedding.
- Streams are embedded from their original source. WildSource does not copy video.
- Credit the camera owner. Provide a link to their site or channel if possible.

## Quality

- The stream should be stable and watchable most of the time.
- Streams that stay offline for an extended period will be hidden by the health check.

## Review outcomes

A reviewer may approve, request changes, or decline a submission, and will say why.
